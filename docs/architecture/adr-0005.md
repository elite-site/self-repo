# ADR-0005 — Do not build the media-service split or CDN now

## Status

Accepted. Decided 2026-09-20.

## Context

Media is stored in Google Drive, and the portal streams it to viewers through the
backend. Traffic is modest (~500 clips per season), so a dedicated pipeline —
upload queue + worker + signed-URL CDN serving — is infrastructure that would be
idle most of the year and hard for a rotating maintainer to reason about.

## Decision

Do not build the media-service split or add a CDN at this time. Keep the existing
Drive streaming proxy: playbacks are proxied through Express, with range-request
support, in `DriveService.streamDriveFile()` in
`backend/src/services/drive.service.ts`. The boundary of a future media service is
carved into that file now — streaming, redirect/URL generation, and Drive file
cleanup are isolated methods with narrow signatures — so a later split is confined
to that module and does not leak into controllers or routes.

## Consequences

- Render proxying of video bytes remains a known ceiling on throughput and egress;
  bandwidth usage counts against the hosting plan.
- No upload queue, worker, or signed-URL logic needs to be operated or explained
  today; the handoff stays small.
- When traffic, events, or egress costs grow past the ceiling, the replacement is a
  contained swap of `drive.service.ts`'s streaming surface rather than a re-architecting
  of the app.
- Delete-media changes are likewise confined: Drive cleanup (failed-upload and
  delete-on-demand) lives beside streaming in the same service, already split into
  per-file/drive-level operations by the boundary noted above.
